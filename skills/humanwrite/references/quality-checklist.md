# Pre-delivery quality check

Apply after drafting and after substantive revision. This review is automatic; do not ask permission to perform it or routinely attach the checklist to the prose. For an essay, the mandatory essay-consistency.md review is part of this check.

## 1. Meaning, evidence, and argument

Check the user's intent, requested scope, language, audience, and constraints. Compare the thesis, paragraph claims, and conclusion; preserve attributed counterarguments and conditional claims. Keep key terms stable rather than rotating near-synonyms. Identify paragraphs that only repeat an assertion and repair missing reasoning with supported material.

Check source-to-claim fit when sources can be inspected. Do not turn citation formatting into a claim of verification. Review distinctive copied wording when source text is available; use faithful paraphrase or attributed quotation. Flag unresolved facts and missing evidence instead of inventing support.

## 2. Natural expression and whole-text structure

Review sentence load, unnecessary subordinating clauses, repetitive connectors, and ornamental words. Use clear vocabulary by default and apply personal style only when enabled. Preserve necessary technical terms and meaningful longer sentences.

Scan for rhetorical em/en dashes, excessive semicolons, colons, quotation marks, and parenthetical asides. Follow ai-writing-patterns.md; preserve quotations and required notation. Review repeated not-X-but-Y constructions, defensive caveats, symmetrical sentence halves, and padded lists of three.

Compare paragraph lengths, openings, reasoning roles, and endings across the whole text. Remove repetitive general-specific-general patterns when they add no reasoning. Let content determine paragraph length; do not manufacture visual irregularity. Replace vague claims with a supported mechanism, bounded example, or analysis, or remove/qualify them when support is unavailable.

## 3. Mode-specific checks

For academic results, use academic-writing.md: prioritize relevant findings without suppressing material null results; explain units, magnitude, and uncertainty; do not narrate every table cell or invent explanations for non-significance. Consolidate caveats while retaining necessary inference limits.

For reflections, use reflection-writing.md: keep events, thoughts, emotions, and actions grounded in supplied experience; connect theory to that experience; develop a concrete proposed action instead of a slogan. Never fabricate hesitation or self-criticism.

For Chinese prose, use chinese-writing.md: check idiom, punctuation, abstract phrasing, repeated sentence patterns, and stable translated terms.

## 4. Repair and deliver

Make changes within the requested scope. Recheck affected argument relationships, terminology, and the conclusion after edits; style changes must not introduce contradictions or remove qualifications. In review-only mode, report specific issues instead of silently rewriting.

Resolve fixable problems before delivery. Briefly disclose material unresolved limitations and label provisional evidence or experience placeholders. Do not claim universal naturalness, detector evasion, originality certification, or a completed behavioral evaluation based on these instructions alone.
