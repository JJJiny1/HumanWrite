# Pre-delivery quality check

Apply to all writing; use the dedicated essay reference for full essay consistency.

- Meaning: preserve the user's intent, audience, language, and requested constraints.
- Substance: each paragraph contributes a claim, explanation, example, or necessary transition. Remove repetition without deleting distinct reasoning.
- Expression: replace empty abstractions and formulaic transitions with precise language. Retain useful technical vocabulary. Do not manufacture casualness or mistakes.
- Accuracy: do not add unsupported facts, invented citations, fake quotations, or experiences. Label unknowns and distinguish internal consistency from source verification.
- Coherence: check sentence relationships, pronouns, terminology, and conclusion scope. Preserve clearly attributed opposing views.
- Style: apply a profile only when enabled; current task instructions take precedence.
- Completion: resolve fixable issues before delivery. Briefly disclose material unresolved limitations; do not attach a routine checklist to clean prose.
